# Content Guide

22Pie copy should sound practical, honest, and developer-friendly.

Use:

- Practical IT training
- Developer-led mentoring
- Interview preparation
- Certification guidance
- CRM consulting
- Freelance software development
- Project delivery

Avoid:

- Guaranteed placement claims
- Unrealistic salary promises
- Fake testimonials, fake instructors, fake clients, or fake metrics
- Generic urgency

Seed content should remain editable through the admin panel.
